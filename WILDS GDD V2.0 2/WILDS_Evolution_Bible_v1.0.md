## HOW EVOLUTION WORKS

Evolution is determined by three inputs that compound over time:

```
DOMINANT STAT TRAINING  +  FOOD DIET  +  HABITAT  =  ADULT FORM
```

The game tracks a rolling average over the last 14 days of play. Early decisions matter, but a player who changes course intentionally can shift their evolution probability if they start early enough in the Juvenile stage.

### The Three Inputs

**1. Dominant Stat Training**
Whichever discipline a player trains most frequently sets their "stat signature." Ties count as balanced.

**2. Food Diet Category**
The food category fed most often (by count) over the Wild's lifetime sets the diet signature. Switched diets matter — if a player feeds Nature food for 5 days then switches to Arcane, the system recalculates.

**3. Habitat**
The habitat the Wild lives in at the time of evolution triggers the habitat modifier. Players who change habitats in the final days before evolution can shift their path.

## CROSS-SPECIES DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Why These Three Work Together for Beta

| Species | Fantasy | Who Plays It |
|---|---|---|
| **Lumi** | The mind that outsmarts | Collectors, puzzle-solvers, INT-path players |
| **Moss** | The anchor that outlasts | Patient players, DEF-builders, long-game thinkers |
| **Cinder** | The force that overwhelms | Aggressive players, PvE-pushers, SPD-STR maxers |

No two players who invest in different habitats + food categories will arrive at the same form, even with the same species. The meta won't consolidate around one "best" build because the elevated and secret forms reward counterintuitive play.

### "Rebirth" Evolution System — The Farewell Connection

Both **Ashroot** (Moss) and **Ash Phoenix** (Cinder) require the Farewell System trigger. This is a design principle: the most emotionally resonant evolutions require the player to have experienced something real in the game. The Ash Seed inheritance item is the mechanical bridge — it is the only item that passes between a dead Wild and the next, and it only appears in two evolution gates.

No player who hasn't lost a Wild can access these forms. This means:
- New players don't know they exist until it's too late (the Wild is already gone)
- Veteran players who experienced the Farewell become the only ones with these forms
- The forms carry visible weight in the social layer — other players see Ashroot or Ash Phoenix and know something about the trainer's history

## EVOLUTION PROBABILITY DISPLAY RULES

How the Evolution Path screen shows probabilities:

1. **All known adult forms** listed with live % based on current stats + diet + habitat
2. **Elevated forms** shown as named entries but with lower % — hint is cryptic but the name is visible once unlocked by first global player
3. **Secret forms** display as `???` with silhouette only and a single-line cryptic hint
4. **Probability for ??? forms** shows as `~X%` (approximate) or `???%` (genuinely unknown)
5. **Live updates** — every training session and feeding updates the % in real time
6. **Tip line** at the bottom — the game surfaces one gentle hint about what Lumi is "responding well to" without giving away the full recipe
